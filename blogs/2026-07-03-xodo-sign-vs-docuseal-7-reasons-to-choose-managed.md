This comparison contrasts Xodo Sign's managed e-signature platform with DocuSeal's open-source, self-hosted alternative. It argues Xodo Sign suits teams wanting vendor-managed infrastructure, built-in compliance, and ready-made templates, while DocuSeal fits technically resourced teams that want direct control.
